Swap HRG ⇄ USDT / USDC

Trade HIDERING peer-to-peer through @HideringOTCBot — an escrow-backed Telegram bot. The bot, operated by HIDERING, holds the HRG in escrow while the buyer pays in stablecoin, then settles both legs. No matching engine and no exchange listing required: buyers pick an open order from /orders.

Open @HideringOTCBot
How it works

Escrow in four steps

01

Post an offer

A seller runs /sell with the HRG amount, the price per HRG, an Ethereum address to receive the USDT / USDC, and an HRG address for refunds. The bot replies with a deposit address; the order goes live in /orders once the HRG deposit has confirmed, and the order is cancelled if the full HRG deposit has not confirmed within 24 hours.

02

Buyer matches

A buyer runs /buy with the order number and an HRG address to receive the coins. The bot replies with an Ethereum deposit address for the order total in USDT or USDC, plus a small amount of ETH for gas. This ETH pays the gas for forwarding the stablecoin to the seller.

03

Bot settles

Once the full order total in one stablecoin (USDT or USDC) and enough gas ETH have arrived at the deposit address, the bot sends the HRG to the buyer (minus the HRG network fee) and forwards the USDT / USDC to the seller's Ethereum address. Each leg is tracked — the attempt is recorded before sending, then its transaction ID — and is designed so that a restart does not resend it.

04

Done

The buyer gets a Telegram message with the HRG transaction ID, and the seller one with the USDT / USDC transaction ID; /status shows both. If the buyer gave an ETH address in /buy, leftover gas ETH is sent back to it on a best-effort basis. A seller can cancel an order while it is listed in /orders; the escrowed HRG is then refunded automatically, minus the network fee.

Commands

Bot commands

Send these to @HideringOTCBot directly. Run /start in the bot for the exact, up-to-date arguments — it lists every command with its current syntax.

/start

Show the full command list with the exact current arguments and the HRG limits per order.

/orders

List the open orders, with their number, HRG amount, price per HRG and total.

/sell

Create a sell order: the amount of HRG, your price per HRG in USDT / USDC, the Ethereum address where you receive the payment, and the HRG address for refunds if you cancel. The bot returns a deposit address for your HRG escrow; send exactly the order amount.

/buy

Reserve an open order and give the HRG address where you will receive the coins. The bot returns an Ethereum address for the USDT / USDC payment plus gas ETH, and tells you how long you have to pay. If nothing has been paid in that time, the reservation is released; an incomplete payment is not released but goes to review by the operator. Optionally add an ETH address so leftover gas can be sent back to you, on a best-effort basis.

/status

Show the state of an order by its number, including the deposit address while it is waiting for a payment and the transaction IDs once it is completed.

/myorders

List your own active orders as a seller.

/cancel

Cancel an order you created while it is listed in /orders. The escrowed HRG is then refunded automatically, minus the network fee — to the address you give with /cancel, or otherwise to the refund address from /sell.

Design

How the desk protects you

Escrow by the operator

The seller's HRG must be deposited with the bot (and confirmed) before an order goes live, and the buyer's stablecoin is paid to a deposit address the bot controls. The bot is operated by HIDERING, which holds both legs until settlement.

Recorded settlement

Each leg is tracked (attempt recorded before sending, then its transaction ID), which is designed to protect against accidental resends after a restart.

Buyer-pays-gas

The on-chain stablecoin gas is paid by the buyer; leftover gas is sent back on a best-effort basis to the ETH address the buyer gave in /buy, if any.

Refund on cancel

While an order is listed in /orders, the seller can cancel it; the escrowed HRG is then returned, minus the network fee, to the refund address the seller gives with /sell or /cancel.

Stablecoin pairs

Trade against USDT or USDC on Ethereum. Pricing is set per order by the seller; a buyer takes an order at its listed total, and the bot settles only once that total has arrived in one of the two stablecoins.

No listing needed

Move HRG in and out without waiting for an exchange listing. Peer-to-peer, directly inside Telegram.

Trade safely

The OTC desk is a peer-to-peer tool — always read the bot's /start output and check the order with /status before sending funds. Double-check addresses and amounts; on-chain transfers are irreversible. Only cancel an order once it is listed in /orders; if you have sent HRG and the order is not listed yet, wait for it to appear before cancelling. Only the official bot at t.me/HideringOTCBot is operated by HIDERING — beware of impersonators. HRG is an experimental asset; never trade more than you can afford to lose.
